# Weather Reporter — User Guide (Web App)

Welcome to **Weather Reporter**, a professional web application designed to provide accurate, real-time weather insights and high-quality reports for any location worldwide.

---

## 1. Key Benefits
*   **Multi-Source Precision:** Toggle between **Open-Meteo**, **MET Norway**, and **OpenWeatherMap** for the most reliable forecasts.
*   **Global Reach:** Search by name, select on an interactive map, or enter precise GPS coordinates (perfect for offshore or remote sites).
*   **Professional Reporting:** Generate detailed weather forecasts in PDF (English/Vietnamese) or professional Excel workbooks with a single click.
*   **Visual Analytics:** Interactive charts for temperature, wind, rain, UV index, and more.
*   **Aesthetic Experience:** Premium dark mode interface with real-time precipitation overlays.

---

## 2. Features & Functionality

### 2.1 Location Selection
Navigate the **Search Location** section using four specialized tabs:
1.  **📋 Saved Locations:** Quickly choose from a list of pre-configured sites (e.g., Rong Doi Platform, An Phu Office).
2.  **🔍 Search:** Type any city or place name (e.g., "Vung Tau") to get suggested coordinates.
3.  **🗺️ Map:** Click anywhere on the interactive map to select a point. Red markers indicate confirmed locations, while blue markers show your pending click.
4.  **📍 Coordinates:** Manually enter Latitude and Longitude for technical precision.

### 2.2 Data Source Selection
Select your preferred weather data provider:
*   **Open-Meteo:** Comprehensive forecast with marine data support.
*   **MET Norway:** High-accuracy European meteorological data.
*   **OpenWeatherMap:** Real-time metrics and medium-range forecasts.

### 2.3 Real-time Dashboard
Displays the current weather at your confirmed location:
*   **Local Time:** Automatically adjusted for the location's timezone.
*   **Core Metrics:** Temperature, Wind Speed (knots), Rain (mm), and UV Index.

### 2.4 Precipitation Map
A live, interactive map powered by Windy.com, showing rainfall overlays for the surrounding region. Includes a custom logo header for a professional look.

### 2.5 Visual Charts
Scroll down to see dynamic, interactive charts for:
*   **Temperature & Humidity**
*   **Wind Speed & Gust** (with knots/direction)
*   **Rain & Probability of Precipitation (PoP)**
*   **Cloud Cover & UV Index**
*   **Marine Data:** Wave/Swell height and period (available via Open-Meteo).

### 2.6 Reporting & Exports
Located at the bottom of the page:
*   **PDF Report:** Choose English or Vietnamese and click "Generate PDF Report" to download a branded weather brief.
*   **Professional Excel:** Detailed data tables and charts formatted for business use.
*   **Historical PDF:** Select a date range to generate a historical weather summary.
*   **Raw CSV:** Export the raw forecast data for your own analysis.

---

## 3. How to Use: Step-by-Step
1.  **Select a Location:** Use the Search or Map tab to find your target site.
2.  **Confirm:** Click "Use this location" or "Confirm coordinates".
3.  **Fetch Data:** Click the **Fetch Data** button to load the latest weather.
4.  **Review:** Explore the dashboard, map, and charts.
5.  **Export:** Scroll to the bottom to generate your PDF or Excel report.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting
*   **"Failed to fetch data":** Verify your internet connection or try a different data source.
*   **Coordinates not updating:** Ensure you clicked "Confirm" after selecting on the map or typing manually.
*   **Marine data missing:** Ensure you are using **Open-Meteo** as the data source.
